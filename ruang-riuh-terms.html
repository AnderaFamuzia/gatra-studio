Terms of Service — Ruang Riuh Uploader

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Ruang Riuh Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Ruang Riuh. It uploads short videos produced by the Ruang Riuh team to Ruang Riuh's own TikTok account.

1. Who can use it. The App is used only by Ruang Riuh and only for the TikTok account(s) that Ruang Riuh owns and has authorized. It is not offered to the public.

2. What it does. After a video has been reviewed and approved by the Ruang Riuh owner, the App sends it to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API, either to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft or, where permitted, as a post with the caption and hashtags prepared by Ruang Riuh.

3. Content. All videos are original content produced by Ruang Riuh. Ruang Riuh is responsible for the content it uploads and for complying with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. TikTok. Use of TikTok through the App is also governed by TikTok's own terms. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

5. Revoking access. The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

6. YouTube. The App also uploads Ruang Riuh's own videos to Ruang Riuh's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, sets their thumbnails, reads that channel's statistics and replies to comments on its own videos. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy; the App is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

7. Changes. These terms may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
