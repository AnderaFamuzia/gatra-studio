Privacy Policy — Ruang Riuh Uploader

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Ruang Riuh Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool that uploads Ruang Riuh's own videos to Ruang Riuh's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the authorizing account (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account the videos go to.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, used only to upload videos to that account.

How it is stored

  • Tokens are stored locally on Ruang Riuh's own computer, outside any public code repository, and are never sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties.
  • The App does not collect data about other TikTok users and does not use TikTok data for advertising or profiling.

What we send to TikTok

  • Video files produced by Ruang Riuh, with their captions and hashtags.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept only while the App is authorized. Revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) stops all access; the locally stored tokens are deleted on request.
